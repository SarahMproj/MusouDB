# Intellectual Property and Asset Policy

> This is a project policy, not legal advice.

## Purpose

MusouDB should be useful to fans while respecting the rights of game publishers, creators, contributors, and players.

MusouDB is unofficial and must not imply that it is produced, sponsored, licensed, or endorsed by KOEI TECMO GAMES CO., LTD. or another publisher.

## Brand practices

- Use original MusouDB branding and interface design
- Display a clear unofficial-project disclaimer
- Use publisher, franchise, game, and character names only as factual references
- Avoid official logos, trade dress, and confusingly similar branding
- Do not call community activities official or sanctioned without written permission

## Material that must not be committed

The public repository must not include:

- Extracted or decrypted game files
- Character models, textures, sprites, maps, or copied UI assets
- Music, sound effects, or voice recordings
- Official promotional artwork without documented permission
- Copied guide, wiki, database, or marketing text
- Rehosted gameplay recordings
- Circumvention tools intended to defeat access controls
- Personal information belonging to another player

## Acceptable contributions

Contributions may include:

- Original software and schemas
- Factual identifiers and community-authored metadata
- Original summaries, comparisons, and analysis
- Public-domain historical information with sources
- Original or properly licensed community artwork
- External links to official public sources
- External links voluntarily added by users to their own profiles or sessions

## Data provenance

Every accepted data contribution should identify its provenance where practical:

- `community_observation`
- `official_public_source`
- `public_domain_history`
- `contributor_original`

Large third-party datasets should not be imported until their licenses and collection methods are reviewed.

## Gameplay links

MusouDB may eventually allow players to link their own streams, videos, or session recaps. The project should store links and user-authored metadata rather than copying the underlying media.

Users must represent that they control or are authorized to share submitted links. Embedding, thumbnailing, and commercial use require separate policy and legal review before launch.

## Community profiles

- Gamertags and external accounts are optional
- Visibility controls must be available per identity
- Users may not impersonate another person or submit another player's personal information
- Profile images must be original, properly licensed, or supplied through an approved identity provider

## Rights-holder requests

The project should maintain a respectful response process:

1. Preserve and review the request.
2. Identify the affected material.
3. Temporarily restrict disputed material when appropriate.
4. Review provenance and permissions.
5. Remove or replace material when required.
6. Record the outcome and improve contributor guidance.

Contributors should never be encouraged to evade legitimate rights-holder requests.
